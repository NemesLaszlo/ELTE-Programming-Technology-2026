### Requirements

The lectures (előadás) are asynchronous online.

The course requirements are:

* **Lecture quizzes:** At least 75% of the lecture quizzes must be completed with a score of **100%**. This requirement also applies to students who have been exempted from attending the lectures.
* **Participation in practical sessions:** Attendance at the practical sessions is required.
* **Assignments:** The assignments given during the practical sessions must be completed and presented by the deadline. Each assignment must receive at least **2 points**. An assignment presentation may be delayed by up to **3 weeks**; however, **1 point is deducted for each week of delay**.
* **End-of-semester computer lab test (ZH):** The end-of-semester practical computer lab test must be successfully completed.
* **Final grade:** The final grade is calculated as the average of the grades received for the assignments and the **computer lab test, which is counted with double weight**. The resulting average is rounded **down if it is below 3.5**.
